\# AI-Assisted Learning Workflow Evaluation



\## Overview



An evaluation framework for responsible adoption of AI-assisted workflows in learning and knowledge development environments.



\## Focus Areas



\- AI Evaluation

\- Responsible AI

\- Human-in-the-Loop Design

\- Learning Technology

\- Workflow Improvement



\## Approach



The framework connects:



AI Assistance → Human Review → Quality Validation → Responsible Adoption



to balance efficiency with accuracy, quality, and accountability.



\## Value



This approach supports teams in:



\- evaluating AI-supported workflows

\- defining review boundaries

\- maintaining human oversight

\- improving responsible adoption practices
